Figure S[x]. Mean ROC curve of the clinical ANN for distant metastasis at 2 years from 5-fold cross-validation, evaluated on the HN-PET-CT training, HN-PET-CT validation and external cohorts. The curve is the sensitivity averaged across the folds with saved weights (HN-PET-CT training: folds 0, 2, 3, 4; HN-PET-CT validation: folds 0, 2, 3, 4; HNC-RADIOMICS external: folds 0, 2, 3, 4).
